Privacy policy

How Social Poster handles your account, your connected social accounts and your content.

1. What we store

  • Your account: name, email address, a one-way hash of your password (never the password itself), your role and preferences.
  • Connected social accounts: the account or Page name, public profile image, and the access and refresh tokens the platform issues. Tokens are encrypted with AES-256-GCM before they are stored.
  • Your content: posts, drafts, schedules, uploaded media and the results of publishing (platform post IDs, errors, timestamps).
  • Engagement figures: views, likes, comments and similar numbers the platforms return for your posts.
  • Activity log: sign-ins, connections, publishing and settings changes, with the IP address used, for security.

2. How we use it

Only to provide the service: to sign you in, publish and schedule the content you create to the accounts you connected, and show you results. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

3. Social platform data

We request only the permissions needed to publish and read basic account and post information. Tokens are used on the server only and are never sent to your browser. Disconnecting an account deletes its stored tokens. You can also remove this app's access from each platform's own security settings at any time.

4. AI features

When you use the AI assistant, the text you provide (and, if you choose, a preview image of your media) is sent to the AI provider configured (OpenAI or Google) to generate suggestions.

5. Cookies

We use one essential, httpOnly session cookie to keep you signed in. It cannot be read by scripts on the page. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

6. Retention and deletion

Your data is kept while your account exists. Deleting your account (Profile and security) permanently removes your posts, schedules, media records, connected accounts and notifications. Uploaded files that no post uses are deleted automatically after a day. Posts already published remain on the social platforms; delete them there if needed.

7. Security

Passwords are hashed with scrypt, sessions use signed tokens in secure cookies, connections use HTTPS, and requests are rate limited. Administrators can see account and activity information needed to operate the service.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact the administrator of this Social Poster installation.
